Corpus Architecture & Balance

The workbench operates on a standardized corpus of N = 50 digital kolam pattern images, curated into two balanced structural classes:

  • Family A (n = 25): Traditional Pulli Kolam grid matrices with symmetrical loop enclosures.
  • Family B (n = 25): Complex Sikku weave topologies featuring continuous non-overlapping paths.
